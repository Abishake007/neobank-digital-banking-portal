Save toward one goal at a time

A pocket is a virtual savings goal linked to your account. For example, create one for a trip, a laptop, or an emergency fund.

  1. Give your pocket a name and set the total amount you want to save.
  2. After it’s created, choose Stash to move money from your available balance into the pocket.
  3. Watch the progress bar as you add money. You can stash funds more than once.
  4. When you reach the target, choose Claim to unlock the saved amount back into your available balance and close the pocket.

Good to know: Stashed money remains part of your account balance, but it is locked from everyday spending while it’s in a pocket. Only stash money you don’t need to spend before reaching the goal.
